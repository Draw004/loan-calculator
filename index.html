HOW IT WORKS

A standard reducing-balance payment is recalculated from principal, rate and term.

For a non-zero monthly rate, the scheduled payment uses the standard amortizing-loan formula. At 0% interest, principal is divided evenly across the term.

Monthly payment / EMIPMT = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1)
Reverse loan amountP = PMT × (1 − (1+r)^−n) / r
IMPORTANT CONTEXT

This is a planning model, not a lender quote.

Real lender figures can differ because of daily-interest conventions, payment dates, fees, variable rates, penalties, rounding and product-specific rules.

  • Country changes familiar terminology; currency changes units only and performs no FX conversion.
  • Entered fees remain separate from principal and interest.
  • Extra payments keep the scheduled payment unchanged; V1 does not model recasting.
  • No prepayment penalty is assumed automatically.
  • Reverse mode estimates mathematical principal only—not lender eligibility or approval.
